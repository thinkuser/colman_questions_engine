## Candidate / program dimensions
All internal scores use a 1–5 relative emphasis scale for the pilot set. These are product-model values, not official academic rankings.

| Dimension | Meaning |

| `data_modeling` | Desire to analyze data, model patterns, predict outcomes |
| `business_context` | Interest in business/management context |
| `math_affinity` | Comfort/interest in substantial mathematics |
| `coding_depth` | Desire for coding to be central to study/work |
| `statistical_thinking` | Interest in probability, statistics, uncertainty, forecasting |
| `systems_process` | Interest in organizational systems and processes |
| `bridge_role` | Desire to connect technology, people, and business |
| `abstract_problem_solving` | Interest in logical/abstract/computational problem solving |

## Positioning shorthand
- **Computer Science:** build the technology.
- **Data Science:** learn and predict from data.
- **Management Information Systems:** use technology/data to solve business and organizational problems.

## Important pair distinctions
### Computer Science vs Data Science
Do not ask simply whether the candidate likes technology. Both are technical. Distinguish primarily between building software/algorithms and discovering/predicting from data.

### Computer Science vs Management Information Systems
Distinguish between technology as the center of the role and technology as a solution inside a business/organization.

### Data Science vs Management Information Systems
Both may involve data, AI, and BI. Distinguish between data/modeling as the profession and data as a tool for business/organizational decisions.
